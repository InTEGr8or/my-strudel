# Old MacDonald

This is the sketch on the Strudel page, written out for the tape trainer. The live-code version sits an octave higher (`F5`) and keeps climbing by semitone; here the **tune is down an octave** so F2–C5 covers melody and bass at keyboard octave 0.

| Section | What to play |
|---------|----------------|
| Verse | `F F F C D D C \| A A G G F` over F, Bb–F, F–C, F |
| Pickup | C–D into the clucks |
| Clucks | Repeated F (“here a … there a …”) over an F drone |
| Verse | Same tune again |
| Up a step | Same verse in F♯ |

Tempo is **140**, same as `.cpm(140/8)` in the sketch. Wait mode: play every note in the column together.
