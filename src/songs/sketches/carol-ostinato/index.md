# Carol of the Bells (ostinato)

The full piano arrangement jumps more than three octaves in one column. This is only the **hook**, written so it stays on the N32 at octave 0.

Right hand: **F4 E4 F4 D4 A3** (the familiar 3/4 figure, one octave below the usual chime). Left hand starts as a **D3** drone, then D3+A3, then a close D-minor triad.

When a column has two or three notes, play them together — same Wait tape as the chord pages.
