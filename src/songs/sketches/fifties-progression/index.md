# I–vi–IV–V in C

The “50s progression” (Heart and Soul, Stand By Me, countless doo-wop tunes). Close position, **F2–C5**, no octave shift.

First two bars are right-hand triads only. Then the same four chords with a left-hand root. The last bar resolves to C instead of G.

| Chord | Function | Right hand | Bass |
|-------|----------|------------|------|
| C | I | C4 E4 G4 | C3 |
| Am | vi | C4 E4 A4 | A2 |
| F | IV | C4 F4 A4 | F3 |
| G | V | B3 D4 G4 | G3 |

Play each column together. That is the next step after single-note lessons.
